01 / Commerce operations

Less preparation.
A deliberate human handoff.

Product setup automation built around the team’s existing Excel-to-Magento workflow.

Title & SKU Research & draft fields Manager review Magento entry

The work that needed to change

Preparing a new product meant gathering source information, writing descriptions and SEO fields, and entering the results into the commerce workflow. The opportunity was to reduce repetitive preparation while keeping the review step clear.

Build around the people doing it

Michael jointly built the n8n workflows with his nephew Josh, coaching him through the work. An Excel row supplied the product title and SKU. Manufacturer documents and web research provided context for generating descriptions, meta titles and other SEO fields.

The output went into designated Excel columns. The ecommerce manager reviewed the material and copied the approved information into Magento.

The automation prepared the information. A person retained the final review and entry.

What this demonstrates

A useful automation can fit the way a team already works. The implementation combined structured output, a clear review responsibility and a familiar platform handoff. Shared development and coaching were part of the delivery.

Evidence and limits

Michael reports a reduction from more than an hour to under ten minutes per product. This is a self-reported comparison, not an independently verified benchmark. The scope of the timing and representative examples have not been independently confirmed.

The case does not establish autonomous publishing or a measured improvement in content quality.
