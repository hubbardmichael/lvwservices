02
Support operations

Internal knowledge assistant

Controlled sources · Staff support

An internal assistant helps staff find product information during calls and emails, drawing on company knowledge, specifications and source documents.

The key decisionSupport the employee handling the enquiry, with defined boundaries for acceptable answers.
Approach & evidence

Michael built the assistant around controlled company product knowledge. Staff remained responsible for the customer conversation. Reported time savings and other benefits are qualitative; no measured sales, returns or accuracy claims are made here.

03
Content operations

Human-reviewed content workflow

Research · Drafting · Editorial decisions

A staged workflow supports research, content planning and drafting. The writer acts as editor and subject-matter reviewer, with decisions at intermediate stages.

The key decisionMake human editorial review a permanent part of the system.
Approach & evidence

The editor can approve, revise or restart work. Michael reports that the workflow was still in use after nearly a year as of October 2026. Continued use is self-reported; no quantified productivity, SEO or revenue gain is claimed.

04
Platform & process architecture

Commerce platform consolidation

Content · Commerce · Migration

Separate content, learning and shopping sites were brought together into one Magento 2 site, addressing platform structure, domain redirection and SEO migration.

The key decisionTreat the migration as an operating change as well as a technology project.
Approach & evidence

Michael describes an initial consulting engagement that consolidated the sites, followed by broader technology and process responsibilities. This case demonstrates platform and operating experience; it carries no AI-runtime or attributed revenue-growth claim.
